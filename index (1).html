Frequently asked questions

Still have a question? Message us on WhatsApp and a designer will reply.

Ask on WhatsApp

How much does interior design cost in Patna?

Costs depend on the size of your space, materials and scope. After a free consultation and site visit, we share a detailed itemised quotation so you know exactly what you are paying for before work begins.

How long does a complete home interior project take?

A typical 2–3 BHK apartment takes 45–60 days from design approval. Villas and commercial projects are planned on a custom timeline, which is written into your agreement.

Is the first consultation really free?

Yes. The first consultation, including a discussion of your requirements and budget, is free with no obligation.

Do you handle execution or only design?

We handle everything end to end: design, 3D visualisation, material selection, carpentry, false ceiling, electrical, painting and final styling, all managed by one team.

Which areas of Patna do you serve?

We work across Patna including Boring Road, Kankarbagh, Danapur, Saguna More, Khagaul Road, Rajabazar, RPS More and nearby areas.

Do you offer a warranty on your work?

Yes. Modular furniture and fittings come with a written warranty, and our team provides after-handover support for any adjustments.
